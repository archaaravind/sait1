ABOUT SAIT

Together Towards
Technology

SAIT is the Students Association of Information Technology, representing and supporting the IT student community at CUSAT. We foster technical excellence, organize national symposiums, and bridge the gap between classroom theory and industry innovation.

Know More
IT Dept SOE CUSAT ∼
Department Foundation

Division of Information Technology

Established under the School of Engineering (SOE) at Cochin University of Science and Technology, the Division of IT offers undergraduate (B.Tech) and research programs engineered to solve complex global technological challenges.

Department Vision

To be an epicenter of excellence in Information Technology education and interdisciplinary research, producing ethical engineers and technological leaders capable of driving global innovation.

Department Mission

Provide an outcome-based academic curriculum, foster state-of-the-art computational infrastructure, promote research in AI, Cloud Computing, and Cyber Defense, and facilitate strong industry-academia collaboration.

AICTE Approved & NBA Accredited Curricula
Specialized High-Performance Compute & IoT Labs
Global Research Collaborations & Publications
Student Association

SAIT — Students Association of IT

SAIT is the autonomous student body governing co-curricular technical initiatives, hackathons, skill-building workshops, and student welfare across all semesters in the IT Division.

SAIT Purpose & Vision

To cultivate an inclusive, high-energy environment where every IT student transforms theoretical knowledge into deployable engineering artifacts, leadership capability, and community contribution.

Journey & Milestones

Key historical achievements shaping the IT Division and SAIT community over the decades.

1995
Founding Era

Department Establishment

Establishment of the Division of Information Technology at School of Engineering, CUSAT, to pioneer computer engineering education in Kerala.

2004
Community Inception

Birth of SAIT

Students Association of Information Technology formally inaugurated to provide an autonomous platform for student technical endeavors.

2014
National Spotlight

Infinitus & National Hackathons

Inception of Infinitus, SAIT's flagship national technology summit, drawing collegiate innovators across India.

2021
