WHO WE ARE

The IT Student Community of CUSAT

SAIT is the Students Association of Information Technology, representing and supporting the IT student community at Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT). Governed by students in close collaboration with faculty mentors, SAIT operates as the central nexus for student connection, technical curiosity, and collaborative learning.
